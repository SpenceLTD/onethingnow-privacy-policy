One Thing Now

Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

One Thing Now keeps the thoughts and progress you save in the app on your device. The app does not send that information to Spence Software or an app server.

Who we are

One Thing Now is published by Spence Software Limited, trading as Spence Software. The company is registered in England and Wales (company number 17539381). Its registered office is 167–169 Great Portland Street, 5th Floor, London, W1W 5PF, United Kingdom.

Information stored by the app

The app does not require an account. It stores information on your device so you can return to an activity where you left off. This can include a thought you choose to park, your current suggested step, recovery progress, and the state of an active focus session and its timer. Text entered on the “Clear my head” screen is saved only if you choose “Park for later.” The app does not transmit this information to the publisher or an app server. It is not a secure notes service, so avoid entering information you would not want stored on your device.

The current version does not include an app account, advertising, or analytics. The publisher does not receive notes or progress stored by the app and does not sell or share that app-stored information. Apple and other providers of your device, operating system, app distribution, or backups may process information under their own privacy policies and settings.

Keeping and deleting app information

Reopening the app restores an active focus session. Stopping the timer stops the countdown but preserves the plan. Finishing a session or choosing a fresh start clears the saved session and recovery state.

A parked thought does not expire automatically. It remains on your device until you remove it in the app or replace it with another parked thought. You can also remove the app and its data using your device’s controls. Device backups may retain app data according to your device and backup settings; manage those backups through your device provider’s settings. Spence Software does not receive those backups.

Clipboard

If you choose to copy a suggested support message, the app places that message on your device’s clipboard. The app does not send the message. You decide whether and where to paste or share it. Clipboard behavior is controlled by your device and the apps you use.

Support email

The app does not send support emails or include your app notes in an email. If you choose to email the publisher, the publisher receives your email address and the information you include so they can respond. Please avoid including sensitive information unless necessary. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed to respond and maintain appropriate business records. The contact mailbox uses Microsoft Outlook.com (Hotmail); Microsoft and any email apps you use may process the correspondence under their own terms and privacy practices.

Visiting this policy page

This page is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for information about GitHub’s practices. This hosting does not mean the app sends your app information to GitHub.

Your choices and privacy rights

You can remove saved app information using the controls described above or by removing the app from your device. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to ask about, access, correct, or delete personal information we hold. Contact us using the address below and we will consider your request under the law that applies.

If you are in the United Kingdom and are concerned about how personal information has been handled, you can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Children’s privacy

The app does not require an account or ask for your name or age. It has free-text fields, so do not enter personal information unless you are comfortable storing it on your device. Parents or guardians should guide children’s use of the app and their device.
